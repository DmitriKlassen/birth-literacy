Common questions

What to know.

Each answer is labelled so you can quickly see which service it applies to. Some questions span more than one kind of support.

Does a doula replace my partner?Birth

No. A doula can help you and your partner feel more prepared and less alone, while offering continuous knowledge and hands-on support to both of you.

Does a doula make medical decisions for me?All services

No. A doula does not speak for you, diagnose, or give medical advice. She can help you understand the conversation, remember your questions, and create room for you to decide.

What does fertility support include?Fertility

Fertility support includes general reproductive education to help you understand reproductive anatomy, menstrual cycles, and fertility. It can also include preparing for appointments, organizing questions, processing options or changing plans, and helping partners communicate through uncertainty. It is non-clinical and shaped around what would be most useful to you.

Can you come to fertility appointments with me?Fertility

Appointment support may depend on location, clinic policies, timing, and availability. Email Christina with the details so you can discuss what is possible and whether another form of preparation or follow-up would fit better.

What is included in birth doula support?Birth

Birth doula care includes prenatal preparation, partner support, practical comfort measures, continuous in-person support through active labour and birth, non-clinical help with communication, and a post-birth follow-up.

Is doula support only for an unmedicated birth?Birth

No. Doula support can be useful across hospital, birth-centre, and home settings, whether or not you plan to use pain medication. The focus is preparation, practical support, and adapting to what unfolds.

What if my birth does not go according to plan?BirthEducation

A birth plan is most useful as a preparation process, not a promise. Knowing your priorities and decision-making questions can still help when circumstances change, and doula support adapts with you.

When will birth education sessions be available?Education

Birth education sessions are planned for later in 2027, with a start date still to be confirmed. The sessions will focus on physiology, informed consent, comfort measures, common interventions, partner preparation, and early postpartum planning.

What can postpartum support look like?Postpartum

Postpartum support is shaped around your home and family. It may include processing the birth, newborn and early-feeding support within doula scope, nourishment, food preparation, rest planning, and help finding a workable family rhythm.

How is food preparation included?Postpartum

Food preparation can be part of practical postpartum care, helping reduce the household load while supporting nourishment and rest. The details are discussed in advance around your family’s needs and preferences.

When should I reach out?All services

You can reach out whenever you begin considering support. Earlier contact allows more time to plan and check availability, but a later conversation can still be useful.

What areas do you serve?All services

Birth Literacy serves Elgin, London, Oxford and surrounding communities in Southwestern Ontario. Contact Christina with your location to confirm availability.
