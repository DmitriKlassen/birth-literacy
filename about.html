Meet Christina Klassen

Support that is steady and genuinely useful.

Christina offers birth and postpartum doula care for families who want to feel informed, cared for, and less alone through pregnancy, labour, birth, and the early days at home.

Her approach is practical, steady, and deeply respectful of each family’s choices. She helps clients understand what is happening in the body during birth, ask clear questions, and participate confidently in decisions as they arise.

Whether she is offering comfort measures in labour, helping a partner feel more prepared and involved, or supporting a family as they settle into postpartum life, Christina brings an attentive presence and a thoughtful, hands-on style of care.

Alongside birth and postpartum support, she offers food preparation for growing families—recognizing how much nourishment, rest, and useful household help matter after birth.

The approach

Clear without being clinical.

Birth Literacy treats you as the primary participant in your own experience. Support is educational without condescension, protective without being combative, and reassuring without being vague.

01 · Understanding

Know what your body is doing.

Physiology becomes practical knowledge you can use, not specialized information held somewhere outside of you.

02 · Language

Ask better questions.

Clear language helps you understand recommendations, name priorities, and create time when decisions feel rushed.

03 · Presence

Stay connected under pressure.

Preparation gives you and your support people concrete ways to reduce panic and return to the next useful step.

04 · Autonomy

Your choices remain yours.

A doula can steady the room and clarify the conversation, but does not take authority away from you or your clinical care team.
