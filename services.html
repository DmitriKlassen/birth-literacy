Services

Choose the support you need.

Fertility, birth, and postpartum support should be specific and useful. Each service begins with a conversation about your circumstances, priorities, and what would genuinely help.

Fertility

Fertility Support

One-to-one support for the practical and emotional demands of navigating fertility care. Christina helps you prepare for appointments, organize questions, process new information, and identify the next useful step.

  • Preparation for appointments and treatment conversations
  • Space to process options, changes, and uncertainty
  • Practical tools for tracking questions and priorities
  • Support for partners navigating the process together

B

Birth Doula Care & Education

Labour, Birth & Preparation

Continuous labour and birth support, grounded in practical preparation before the day arrives. Christina helps you understand what is happening, prepare your partner, find workable comfort measures, and make space for questions when decisions arise.

  • Prenatal conversations about hopes, concerns, and care preferences
  • Labour physiology and informed-consent language
  • Comfort measures, positioning, rest, and partner roles
  • Steady in-person presence through active labour and birth
  • Clear, non-clinical support while you communicate with your care team
  • Post-birth follow-up and space to process your experience

Postpartum & Family

After-Birth Support

Practical, steady support as your family settles in after birth. Care is shaped around what reduces the load in your actual home—whether that is processing the birth, making food, finding a workable rhythm, or getting grounded help with early feeding.

  • Space to talk through and make sense of the birth
  • Practical newborn and early-feeding support within doula scope
  • Rest, nourishment, and family rhythm planning
  • Food preparation for the early days at home

How it works

A clear place to begin.

You do not need to know exactly which service or level of support you need before reaching out. The first conversation is for sorting that out.

  1. 01

    Reach out

    Share your due date, location, and the kind of help you are considering.

  2. 02

    Talk it through

    Use a consultation to name priorities, questions, and practical needs.

  3. 03

    Choose support

    Decide what fits your family without pressure or unnecessary extras.

  4. 04

    Prepare together

    Build understanding and a workable plan before support begins.

Not sure where to start?

Send a short note about where you are in pregnancy and what feels most uncertain. We can begin there.
